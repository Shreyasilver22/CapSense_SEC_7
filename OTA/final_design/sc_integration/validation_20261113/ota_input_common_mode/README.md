# Input common-mode characterization of the final SCL08 OTA

The fixture uses the final extracted-capacitance OTA, nominal 5 V supply, 27 °C, typical SCL08 MOS and RPP models, output common-mode setpoint 2.5 V, 5 pF per output, VBN = 3.0 V, VBP = VDD − 2.2 V and the 4.5 µA OTA reference current from the integrated SC candidate. A supplemental 3.5 µA sweep is included in the nominal CSVs. The fixture holds input common-mode fixed and provides ideal differential feedback for transient measurements.

- Gain is differential open-loop magnitude at 0.01 Hz. The fixture balances the outputs at DC and opens the external feedback path for AC. Unity-gain frequency and phase margin are measured at the first downward unity crossing.
- Device saturation margin is native BSIM3 |VDS| − |VDSAT|. A positive margin indicates saturation for the monitored biased device. Per-device CSVs include VDS, VDSAT, VGS and absolute drain current for both input transistors, four cascodes, both PMOS loads, the tail and its reference mirror. VDD current includes IREF.
- The differential command moves from minus A to plus A over 10 ns, so each output moves A. Settling is the last entry of both outputs into bands of ±0.1% of A around their respective final plateau. Differential target settling is tracked separately. The nominal window is 40 µs per step. Selected low-CM reruns use 220 µs per step. NaN in a settling field means the waveform failed to reach and stay in the band before the end of its window.
- The nominal 4.5 µA DC headroom crossings are near 1.122 V, limited by the NMOS tail, and 2.838 V, limited by the NMOS input pair. These are DC crossings with output centered at 2.5 V. The transient sweep includes a 2.5 V input common-mode baseline.
- The selected corner tests include the four alternate MOS cards at 5 V and 27 °C, hot bnwp at 5.5 V and 85 °C with worst RPP, and cold wnwp at 4.5 V and 0 °C with best RPP. The cold case gives about +6 mV of tail margin at 1.40 V input common-mode and +76 mV at 1.50 V.

`nominal_summary.csv` combines 4.5 µA small-signal and settling results. `nominal_devices.csv` has transistor operating points, and `corner-boundary_transient.csv` has dynamic margins for selected low-end corners. Run a deck in `benches/` with ngspice in a writable directory. Decks use local model paths and may emit inherited HSPICE-parameter warnings.

Run `python3 sweep.py /tmp/ota_icmr --group nominal` from this directory. Other groups are `corners`, `boundary`, `fine` and `corner-boundary`. Run `python3 plot.py /tmp/ota_icmr` after the nominal and boundary groups finish. The 80 ns low-CM retry data are retained separately. Initial 5 ns dynamic diagnostic runs reached their time limit. The 8 ns reruns completed and corroborated the 20 ns measurements near the lower operating boundary.
