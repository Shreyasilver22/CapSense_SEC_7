# SC sensor plate references at 2.5 V

This study changes the switched analog references in the released 10 kHz SC circuit. RX clamp is the reference during sample/reset. TX discharge is the reference during transfer. The four variants use 0/0, 2.5/0, 0/2.5, and 2.5/2.5 V at those terminals. OTA VSS, transistor bodies, voltage-source returns, and the low level of the clocks use physical ground. A separate control also returns the 5 pF output loads to 2.5 V. The nominal circuit retains 40 pF per feedback branch, 5 V excitation, 1 µs edges, and 1 µs RX lead. Samples are taken at 80 µs within the cycle.

At equal 12 pF sensors, the original input and output common-mode minima were −0.552 V and 0.682 V. With both switched references at 2.5 V, they were 1.633 V and 2.088 V. The sampled averages moved from 0.782 V and 2.500 V to 1.925 V and 2.499 V. At 12/7 pF the differential signal moved from 756.38 mV to 252.79 mV. Moving only TX discharge gives 505.02 mV, with input and output minima of 1.101 V and 2.028 V at 12/12 pF. The RX-only modification gives 505.67 mV but retains the large output common-mode dip.

For ideal charge balance, define E = VDD − V_RX,clamp + VCM − V_TX,discharge. Then Vdiff = 2E(CP − CN)/(2CF + CP + CN) and Vinput,CM = VCM − E(CP + CN)/(2CF + CP + CN). The effective excitation E is 7.5 V originally, 5 V with one reference moved, and 2.5 V with both moved. With CN held at 7 pF, a ±0.05 pF perturbation around CP = 12 pF gives 140.03 mV/pF originally and 47.98 mV/pF with both moved.

The full 36-pair nominal grid for both switches is split between endpoints.csv and grid.csv. At 12/7 pF the reference sinks about 99 µA peak with both switched references moved, versus 77 µA for TX-only and 309 µA for RX-only. With both moved, a series reference resistance of 100 Ω or 1 kΩ produces about 9.5 mV or 85.7 mV peak reference movement, respectively. At ideal reference impedance, moving the output load returns to VCM leaves the transient voltages effectively unchanged. The reference remains an ideal source with optional series resistance. No physical driver or ADC is attached.

Run python3 study.py to regenerate the transient results, and python3 plot.py to generate the comparison figure. The SPICE files use the installed SCL08 models and the final capacitance-extracted OTA. Sensor noise is estimated by perturbing the charge on each sensor independently in the clocked circuit. It covers the sensor thermal contribution, and total sampled noise remains open.

All 16 selected MOS/supply/temperature stress runs for the both-at-2.5 V variant completed. The lowest input and output common-mode excursions were 1.305 V and 1.801 V. The largest differential settling time was 9.67 µs and the largest sampled error from ideal charge balance was 0.365 mV in that subset. The ideal reference had to sink up to 373 µA. The nominal 12/7 pF timestep and cycle cross-check changed the differential sample by less than 1 µV. Sensor-only output differential noise was 6.90 µV RMS originally and 6.93 µV RMS with both references moved. Dividing by the corresponding local sensitivities gives roughly 49 aF and 144 aF RMS sensor contributions.
