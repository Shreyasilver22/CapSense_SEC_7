# SCL08 PMOS feedback capacitors for the 10 kHz SC readout

This test replaces both ideal 40 pF feedback capacitors in the TX-only bench. TX switches to 2.5 V during transfer, the RX sampling clamp remains at ground, and the final capacitance-extracted telescopic OTA sets its output common-mode near 2.5 V. Conditions are 5 V, 27 °C, typical models, 5 pF output load per branch, and the 80 µs sample unless otherwise specified.

One PMOS unit is W = 20 µm and L = 10 µm with source, drain, and body shorted. Incremental capacitance is measured at 10 kHz as the imaginary part of the gate current divided by angular frequency. The PMOS model gives about 412.55 fF per unit on the gate-positive accumulation side. The selected bank uses 97 units per branch for about 40.02 pF, with the gate at the SC output and the other three terminals at the corresponding summing node. The ideal reference and both alternative bank orientations and sizing methods are in screen.csv. Sizing at one bias in the opposite polarity gives a misleading result because the capacitance can fall near 90 fF per unit in the depletion region.

At CP/CN = 12/7 pF the selected bank gives 504.845 mV versus 505.022 mV with ideal feedback. Local sensitivity with CN fixed is 95.788 mV/pF versus 95.820 mV/pF. Differential settling within 0.1% of the 88 µs sample is 7.28 µs versus 7.29 µs. Output common-mode at the sample is 2.49723 V. At 12/12 pF the minimum input and output common-mode voltages are 1.102 V and 2.028 V. The sensor-only sampled differential noise is 6.922 µV RMS versus 6.925 µV RMS using the same charge perturbation method. This does not include OTA, switch, reset or MOS bank noise.

The gate area is 19,400 µm² per branch. This is a schematic bank made from native transistor models with estimated diffusion geometry. A dedicated isolated n-well tied to the summing node is implicit in the MOS terminal connections. Well-to-substrate capacitance and leakage, interconnect, matching, and physical capacitor model validity need to be verified in a layout implementation.

Run python3 study.py, python3 qualify.py, python3 noise.py and python3 plot.py in this folder. The scripts use the earlier TX reference study and shared SC testbench. The runnable reversed_12_7.cir is the gate-at-output circuit.

All 36 nominal sensor pairs completed. The largest positive plate-minus-gate voltage was under 0.74 mV across that grid, so the bank remains on or very near its accumulation side. The four selected model/supply/temperature combinations at two sensor pairs gave eight matched ideal-versus-MOS comparisons. At 12/7 pF the largest difference from ideal feedback was 40.96 mV (7.37%), in the best-N/best-P model at 5.5 V and 0 °C. The gate-at-output bank is a viable nominal candidate, with process-dependent conversion gain that must be budgeted or calibrated. The nominal 10 ns timestep rerun shifts the sample by less than 1 µV.
