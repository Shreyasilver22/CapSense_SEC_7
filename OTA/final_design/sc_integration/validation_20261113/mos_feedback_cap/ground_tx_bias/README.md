# Ground-referenced TX and OTA reference current

The 10 kHz SC testbench uses TX = 0 V during transfer, a grounded RX sampling clamp, and 97 parallel 20 µm × 10 µm PMOS devices per feedback branch (about 40 pF at nominal bias). The final capacitance-extracted OTA retains VBN = 3.0 V and VBP = VDD − 2.2 V. Its common-mode target is 2.5 V. Both output loads are 5 pF. Nominal runs use typical SCL08 models at 5 V and 27 °C. The sample is 80 µs into the second cycle.

The current sweep varies IREF from 2.5 to 9 µA. Local sensitivity is measured at 12/7 pF with CN fixed. Settling is measured from 46 µs against a band of 0.1% of the differential value at 88 µs or 100 µV, whichever is larger. Main rail power includes the OTA and SC on VDD and excludes clock and reference generation. Junction current is measured in the RX-facing NMOS bulk junctions of the clamp and receive switches on both branches. The reported charge is the sum of the absolute currents integrated from 45 to 55 µs. This is a diagnostic of the selected junctions rather than a direct measure of net lost sensor charge or a reliability limit.

At 12/7 pF, increasing IREF from 3.5 to 9 µA changes sensitivity from 140.00 to 143.34 mV/pF, settling from 7.28 to 5.75 µs, and main rail power from 242 to 550 µW. At 12/12 pF, the minimum OTA input average improves from −0.551 to −0.475 V and the lowest RX voltage improves from −0.618 to −0.543 V. The peak selected junction current decreases from 0.273 to 0.015 µA, and integrated absolute charge decreases from 13.34 to 0.77 fC. Thus increasing current can reduce charge disturbance while the below-ground excursion persists.

Run python3 sweep.py, python3 sensor_noise.py, then python3 plot.py in this directory. The scripts reuse the previous PMOS feedback and TX reference studies. Total sampled noise, full PVT, physical resistor/capacitor layout effects, and a real ADC load are outside this bench.

The selected corner screen compares 3.5 and 9 µA at 12/7 and 12/12 pF across four MOS/supply/temperature combinations. The worst RX voltage goes from −0.765 to −0.743 V and the maximum selected-junction charge goes from 1.30 to 0.40 pC. Worst absolute 12/7 pF error relative to ideal charge balance falls from 168 to 135 mV. This error includes process-dependent MOS feedback capacitance, headroom, and switching effects. It cannot be assigned entirely to the junction current. The increased current does not qualify the 0 V option across corners.
